Resolution layer for prediction markets

Settle every market on evidence anyone can check.

Three models read the same locked evidence inside sealed hardware and sign their answers. When all three agree, the market settles. When they don't, Verdict refuses to guess and hands the market to the arbiter the app chose.

Why

Resolution is the part nobody should own.

Apps keep what they're good at: market creation and trading. Verdict only resolves. There is no single human resolver to trust or pressure, just three independent models, locked evidence, and signatures the contract checks.

Answers

Every market gets one of three.

The contract does not average the models. It settles, hands the market back, or waits for the app's arbiter.

Final

Unanimous, then quiet.

All three models sign the same YES or NO, and nobody disputes before the window closes.

Escalated

Split, or ambiguous.

The models disagree, or they all answer AMBIGUOUS. Verdict does not fill in an outcome. The app's escalation_owner decides.

Disputed

The app's arbiter.

During the window, anyone can challenge the proposed outcome with a bond. That challenge goes to the same escalation_owner.

How it works

Six steps, all on-chain.

01
CreateThe app creates a market with its sources and its escalation_owner.
02
LockA relayer fetches the sources and commits the evidence hash.
03
VoteThree models vote on NEAR AI Cloud sealed hardware and sign.
04
CheckThe contract checks each signature against the registered signers.
05
ProposeA unanimous, clear outcome is proposed and the dispute window opens.
06
FinishNo dispute, and it is final. A split, an ambiguous panel, or a dispute goes to the app's escalation_owner.

// read status, votes and the final outcome
get_market(id)

// anyone can challenge during the window, with a bond
