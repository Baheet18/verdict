How it works

  1. 01 Create market The integrating app calls create_market on verdict-v2-baheet.testnet and passes its own escalation_owner.
  2. 02 Lock evidence After the deadline, a registered relayer calls commit_evidence. The bundle is pinned to a commit SHA, not /master/.
  3. 03 Three TEE-bound models vote Qwen3.8-27B, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, and GLM-5.3-flash. Each vote is submit_verdict.
  4. 04 Proposed The contract proposes an outcome only when all three agree. A split, or unanimous AMBIGUOUS, becomes Escalated.
  5. 05 Dispute window 1 hour on market 0. 10 minutes on market 1. A challenge in the window is dispute with the bond attached.
  6. 06 Final, or escalate With no dispute, finalize closes the market. Otherwise the integrating app’s escalation_owner settles that market.

For apps

  1. Pass escalation_owner at create_market That account is the only one that may call resolve_dispute for this market. It can be your team, a multisig, a DAO, or a jury contract. The protocol owner settles a dispute only when you pass that account as escalation_owner.
  2. Wait for Proposed or Final Read get_market. Deposits and claims stay in your app. The relayer commits evidence, collects the three votes, and calls finalize when the window closes with no dispute.
  3. Handle dispute During the window, a challenger calls dispute with the bond. Disputed and Escalated stay with that market’s escalation_owner.

Limits

  • 107 of 124 post-cutoff sports settled and matched UMA. 17 escalated. 0 invented answers on unclear markets. The set is easy final-score markets. docs/BENCHMARK.md
  • Demo dispute window: 1 hour on market 0, 10 minutes on market 1.
  • prompt_template_hash on market 1 is fe85745ffa51fc2ac8a80b61fff3e13c3e680f4c2cf71f6142f10b5bd97f7d8f, the sha256 of the versioned prompt in scripts/verdict_request.py.
  • A registered relayer chooses and commits the evidence snapshot. It cannot edit a vote the contract has already accepted. This is not a trustless oracle.
  • Explorer links are the on-chain record. This page does not verify signatures or hardware attestation. Hardware checks are an off-chain precondition for signer registration.
  • baheet.testnet is the old prototype (universal protocol-owner dispute fallback). The current contract is verdict-v2-baheet.testnet.
